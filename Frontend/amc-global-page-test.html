🚀 Usage Instructions

To use this shell in your pages:

  1. Include amc-globalshell.css in your <head>
  2. Add sidebar container: <div id="sidebar-container"></div>
  3. Add header placeholder: <div class="amc-header"></div>
  4. Load scripts: sidebar.js, amc-header.js, auth.js
  5. Your content goes in <main class="main-content">
