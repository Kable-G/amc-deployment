Logo Usage Guidelines

  • Pattern: 2×2 grid of rectangles (wider than tall)
  • Top Left: Black filled rectangle
  • Top Right: White rectangle with black border
  • Bottom Left: White rectangle with black border
  • Bottom Right: Black filled rectangle
  • Border Radius: Subtle rounding (3-8px depending on size)
  • Aspect Ratio: Approximately 1.6:1 (width:height)
